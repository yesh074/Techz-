# TEZLOGI AI --- Industrial AI-Powered Predictive Logistics

> **Predict Before You Procure.**

TEZLOGI AI is an intelligent institutional resource and logistics
management platform designed to ensure that educational resources are
available at the **right place, at the right time, and in the right
quantity**.

Unlike a traditional inventory system, TEZLOGI AI combines institutional
resource management with **AI-powered demand forecasting, shortage
prediction, smart procurement, supplier intelligence, delivery tracking,
and MARCO AI assistance**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚀 Project Vision

Educational institutions manage thousands of resources across
departments, laboratories, libraries, classrooms, and digital platforms.

TEZLOGI AI continuously analyses:

-   Historical resource usage
-   Student enrollment
-   Department-wise demand
-   Academic schedules
-   Examination periods
-   Semester cycles
-   Seasonal demand
-   Procurement history
-   Supplier lead times
-   Inventory levels
-   Resource utilization
-   Maintenance schedules

The system uses these signals to predict future requirements and
proactively recommend actions.

### Core Question

TEZLOGI AI should not only answer:

> "What resources do we have?"

It should answer:

> **"What will we need, when will we need it, what might run out, what
> should we procure, who should supply it, and when will it arrive?"**

# 🤖 Forecasting Architecture

The forecasting engine should be modular.

Start with explainable statistical forecasting where appropriate, such
as:

-   Moving average
-   Weighted moving average
-   Trend analysis
-   Seasonal analysis

Structure the forecasting service so it can later support:

-   Random Forest
-   XGBoost
-   Prophet
-   ARIMA
-   LSTM
-   Other time-series models

Do not hard-code fake AI predictions.

Keep the model/service abstraction separate from the UI and database.
